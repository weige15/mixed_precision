# OWQ: Outlier-Aware Weight Quantization for Efficient Fine-Tuning and Inference of Large Language Models

**Authors:** Changhun Lee, Jungyu Jin, Taesu Kim, Hyungjun Kim, Eunhyeok Park  
**Year:** 2023 / AAAI 2024  
**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.02272  
**AAAI paper:** https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/download/29237/30335  
**Project:** https://github.com/xvyaward/owq

## Key Idea

OWQ keeps a small set of quantization-sensitive structured weights in higher precision while quantizing the rest more aggressively. It also includes a weak-column tuning path for efficient task adaptation.

## Relevance

OWQ is close to the "selective rescue" framing: low precision is the default, and sensitive columns are protected.

## Connection To Current Project

- Strong precedent for selective high-precision exceptions.
- Suggests the H7 predictor may need finer granularity than modules, such as columns/channels.
- Weak-column tuning is relevant to LoRA-style adaptation because both target small trainable subsets under a frozen or mostly frozen backbone.

## Limitations For This Project

OWQ is mostly weight-quantization-oriented. The current project observes module-output perturbation and LoRA training outcomes, so direct method transfer would require new labels and kernels.
