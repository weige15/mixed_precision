# ARKV: Adaptive and Resource-Efficient KV Cache Management under Limited Memory Budget for Long-Context Inference in LLMs

- Authors: Jianlong Lei, Shashikant Ilager
- Year: 2026
- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08727
- Code: https://github.com/Large-scale-Sustainable-Computing-LSC/ARKV
- Topic: Adaptive KV-cache retention, quantization, and eviction

## Key Idea

ARKV assigns cached tokens to three runtime states: Original full precision,
Quantization low precision, or Eviction. It estimates per-layer
original-versus-quantized ratios from attention entropy, variance, and kurtosis
during prefill, then ranks tokens during decoding with a heavy-hitter scoring
strategy.

## Why It Is Like MoBiQuant

MoBiQuant routes token-dependent weight bit slices; ARKV routes token-dependent
KV-cache states. ARKV is even closer to soft pruning vocabulary because its
state space explicitly includes full precision, low precision, and deletion.

## Fit To Soft Pruning

This is a direct mixed precision plus soft-pruning bridge:

```text
Original = high-bit keep
Quantization = low-bit keep
Eviction = 0-bit prune
```
